No link isn't disqualifying — it just means the claim goes to "needs evidence" instead of "verified," with a specific reason shown, not a score.

This demo checks whether a link was provided and looks like a plausible source — it does not fetch or read the page. A real backend would fetch the link, look for corroborating content, and still route every result to human review.
